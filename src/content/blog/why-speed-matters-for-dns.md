## The Hidden Cost of Slow DNS

When a user clicks a link, their browser cannot load a single image, script, or stylesheet until the DNS resolution completes. If your DNS takes 200ms to resolve, you've already lost the attention of a percentage of your users.

### The Amazon Study

Classic studies by Amazon and Google have proven that every 100ms of latency costs 1% in sales. For an enterprise e-commerce platform, that translates to millions of dollars left on the table simply because the initial DNS lookup was slow.
